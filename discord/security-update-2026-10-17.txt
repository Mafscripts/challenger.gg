**TOPFRAGG — SECURITY UPDATE**

Cheaters are not welcome at Topfragg. Fair competition comes first. **Five users have already received permanent bans.**

**Stronger account and access security**
• Email verification is required before full account access, with limits on login and registration attempts.
• Improved visitor IP recording and linked-account checks help staff investigate ban evasion. A shared IP alone is not proof of cheating or multiple-account abuse.
• VPN, proxy and Tor checks cover registration, login and authenticated access. Connections detected as these services are blocked. Detection cannot identify every VPN or prevent every attempt at ban evasion.

**How permanent bans work**
• A permanent ban has no automatic expiry. Access stays restricted unless staff explicitly removes the ban.
• Changing your IP or switching to a VPN does not lift the ban on your existing account.
• Depending on the enforcement action, restrictions can cover the account, its email address and its recorded IP addresses. An IP ban can also affect other accounts using those addresses.
• Banned profiles display a red **BANNED** pill. Restricted players see a dedicated screen explaining their ban reason and duration.

**Private appeals and staff accountability**
• Banned players can message the CEO team directly from their ban screen. Only that player and CEOs can read and reply to the conversation.
• Sending an appeal does not automatically remove a ban. Closing an appeal conversation does not unban the account.
• Moderation actions require a written reason and are recorded in the audit log. Wallet adjustments are restricted to CEOs.

Suspect cheating or another rule violation? Submit a ticket with the relevant match details and evidence so staff can review it.

**Play fair. Respect the community. Cheating has no place here.**
