TOPFRAGG - HOW TO JOIN FREE 8s ON DESKTOP
Actual website interface at 2560 x 1440, with example account and match data.
Discord authorization happens in Discord; no Discord login is impersonated in this guide.

1. Open Account Settings
Open your profile menu, then click Account Settings. This is the actual desktop website.

2. Connect Discord
Account Settings > Connections > Discord: connect, authorize on Discord and join the Topfragg server. Sync role if needed.

3. Save your Activision ID
Connections > Gaming IDs > Edit: enter your full Activision ID, including #numbers where applicable, then Save Changes.

4. Choose your game rank
Account > Game rank: Titan = Diamond, Master = Crimson, Apex = Iridescent. Legend = Top 250 and requires admin approval.

5. Find a Free 8s lobby
Open Free 8s, choose your game and switch to Matchfinder. Check 2v2 / 3v3 / 4v4 and the game mode, then Accept This Match.

6. Join this match's Waiting Room
In the matchroom, click Join 8s Waiting Room. Connect in Discord with your linked account and stay in this match's room.

7. Teams and voice are automatic
Once the lobby is full and everyone is in its Waiting Room, teams are assigned and the bot moves players to team voice. Follow the matchroom's maps and instructions.

8. Using the sidebar? Same setup.
The sidebar has the same Free 8s and Account Settings links. Your Discord, Activision ID and game rank setup carries across both layouts.
